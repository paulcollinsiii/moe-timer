## CLEAN_BANDS (display.c) — unchanged, and why

The table is the main screen's geometry and the four bands jointly cover every
byte row (bytes 0..2, 3..6, 7..10, 11..15 = rows 0..23, 24..55, 56..87,
88..127). Mapping of break elements:

| band | rows (bytes) | covers |
|------|--------------|--------|
| 0 {3,17}   | 0..23   | title (static, never dirty) |
| 1 {26,49}  | 24..55  | top of the countdown (26..55) |
| 2 {58,87}  | 56..87  | bottom of the countdown (56..59), draining bar (68..83) |
| 3 {95,125} | 88..127 | chore line (89..104), bottom row / footer (109..124) |

The figure is static between partials, so it needs no band. Pinned by
`test_the_break_elements_sit_inside_the_clean_bands_that_cover_them`, which
reads the real table out of main/display.c.

## Decisions

- Countdown: m:ss/mm:ss under 3600 s, h:mm:ss from 3600 s, 48 pt, centred in
  the column; fits at every tested value incl. 2:00:00 (>= 2 px of margin to
  either column edge).
- Chores line: "Chores 1 of 3" + "Screen 20:00" = 211 px > 192, so shortened to
  "Chores 1/3" (the plan's fallback). Even shortened the worst boxes abut at 10 h+
  of frozen screen time (gap 0..3 px, never overlapping); under 10 h the gap is
  >= 9 px. Pinned in two tiers; flagged to the coordinator.
- Chore line y = 88 (not 89): '/' paints 89..104 and the gutter to the button row
  must stay >= 4.
