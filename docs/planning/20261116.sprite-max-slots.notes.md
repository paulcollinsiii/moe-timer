# Sprite slots at their max size — working notes (2026-11-16)

Branch `feat/sprite-max-slots` from `integration`.

## Measured room (goldens, ink outside today's art box)

Rule applied: the art BOX keeps >= 4 px to every other element's ink, and
>= 2 px off any panel edge it does not deliberately bleed (the fit tests'
rule: `test_the_charge_me_screen_fits_the_panel`, `..._timesup_..._bleed`,
`..._bedtime_screen_fits_the_panel` all require 2 px clear). Exceptions:
break keeps only 2 px above its bottom row's ink (y 110 under the art's
columns, box ends y 107); timesup runs to the bottom edge (the bleed).

| slot | scale | box origin | old | max | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| break | 2 | (2,2), was (4,4) | 46x52 | 47x53 | bar ink x100 = 4 px past box x95; bottom-row ink under the art y110 = 2 px below box y107 (the 2x step cannot reach 4 there). Left and top: one art px (2 panel px) to the 2 px edge margin, no other ink at x<4 / y<4. |
| bedtime | 4 | (4,4) | 29x23 | 32x25 | "Bed Time"/"Goodnight!" ink starts x137, bottom line y111. 32 -> box x4..131 (5 px gutter; 33 -> 1). 25 -> y4..103 (7 px; 26 -> 3). Left/top: one art px is 4 panel px, off the panel. |
| charge | 1 | (2, centred), was x8 | 105x122 | 154x124 | words ink x160 -> right edge x155. Left: the 2 px edge rule -> x2 (no other ink at x<8). Height 124 -> y2..125. |
| timesup | 1 | (2, bottom flush) | 178x124 | 187x126 | title ink x193 -> box x2..188. Top: fit test forbids ink at y<2 -> box y2..127 (h 126, not 128). |

First pass (b836f3f) stopped charge at 148 wide at x8 and break at 46x52 at
(4,4), the corners the template art happened to start at; the review found
both could grow left (and break up) to the 2 px edge margin. Fixed by moving
CHARGE_ART_X 8 -> 2 and BREAK_ART_X/_Y 4 -> 2 and padding the templates on
the left (and top) so their ink stays on the same panel pixels.
`test_no_lock_art_box_can_grow_by_one_art_pixel` now fails if any slot could
grow one art pixel on any side and still keep the rules.

## Placement of a smaller image (gen_sprites.pad_offset)
- vertical anchor (SLOT_ANCHOR): break, bedtime top; charge centred
  ((H-h)//2 above, odd row below; 2 + (124-h)//2 == (128-h)//2 for every h);
  timesup bottom
- left/top inset (SLOT_INSET), art px, each clamped to the room the image
  leaves: charge (6, 0) -> a 148-or-narrower image starts at x8 as before;
  break (1, 1) -> a 46x52-or-smaller image starts at (4,4) as before. The
  inset is exactly the slot's growth past the old art corner, so owner art
  drawn at any old size lands on the same panel pixels.

## Decisions
- bedtime stays 32x25. A 26th row fits only at BEDTIME_ART_Y 3 (y 3..106,
  4 px to y111), which moves the template art up 1 panel px (or 3 down if
  padded a row on top): the golden changes. Recorded in the bedtime comment
  in display_screens.c.
- Not checked on hardware: custom bedtime art that fills the box puts white
  ink 5 px from the 28 pt white words (the template's ink stops 17 px away).
  White-on-black bleed at that distance has not been looked at on the panel.

## Layout literals
TIMESUP_COL_X / CHARGE_COL_X / BEDTIME_COL_X are LABEL columns with centred
text; the grown boxes now reach into them. Moving them would re-centre the
words and change goldens, so they stay, and the old `ART_X + w <= COL_X`
assertions became ink tests (words' ink >= box right + 4) plus
`test_the_lock_art_boxes_keep_a_gutter_to_every_other_element`.
