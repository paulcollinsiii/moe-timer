## Task 1: the new defaults

`MAGTAG_SWAP_COUNT_QUIET_MS` 700 -> 1000 (range 200..3000) and
`MAGTAG_SWAP_COUNT_BUDGET_MS` 6000 -> 9000 (range 500..10000). Both inside
their ranges.

Worst case, from the `SWAP_COUNT_WORST_*` macros in `main/wake_flow.c` (the
quiet window enters none of them; the budget is the only swap term):

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| `WAKE_FLOW_EVENT_WINDOW_MS` | 15 + 15 + 5 + 3 + 3 + 1.5 = 42.5 s |
| `WAKE_FLOW_WORST_PAINT_MS` | 4 s |
| `WAKE_FLOW_BREAK_ALERT_MS` (3 cycles) | (3*22 + 10) * 100 = 7.6 s |
| tail = max(2 * 4, 7.6 + 4) | 11.6 s |
| `WAKE_FLOW_GRID_WAIT_MAX_SEC` | 25 s |

| Assert | Limit | Old (6000) | New (9000) | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gesture = budget + tail + window | 90 s (half of 180) | 60.1 s | 63.1 s | 26.9 s |
| EXT1 = window + gesture | 180 s | 102.6 s | 105.6 s | 74.4 s |
| tick = 2 windows + grid + paint + gesture | 180 s | 174.1 s | 177.1 s | 2.9 s |

All three `_Static_assert`s hold. The tick path is the binding one: the
largest budget it admits at a 180 s failsafe is 11.9 s, above the Kconfig
range's 10 s ceiling, so every value the range allows builds.

The build cannot check this here (no idf.py on this task); the arithmetic
above is the evidence, and the host build compiles the same asserts at the
test figures (410 / 1750).

The owner's `sdkconfig` carries 700/6000 lines that are `# default:`-marked,
so a reconfigure recomputes them to 1000/9000. Not touched here; check with
`grep SWAP_COUNT sdkconfig` after `idf.py reconfigure` before flashing.

## Task 2: does the wrap-around have a defect?

Verdict: NO DEFECT in the wrap-around. Path followed end to end:

- `wake_flow_swap_count_gesture` (main/wake_flow.c ~1631): candidate starts at
  `timer_slot_after(timer_active_slot())`, every taken C press steps it with
  `timer_slot_after(candidate)`, the loop ends on quiet window or budget, then
  `timer_select_slot(candidate)` once.
- `timer_slot_after` (main/timer.c:193): `(slot + i) % TIMER_SLOT_COUNT` over
  i = 1..COUNT-1, first enabled wins; `slot_enabled(0)` is always true
  (timer.c:53), so from the last enabled timer it returns Screen, and from
  Screen the first enabled extra. It never returns the slot it was given and
  never returns -1 while an extra exists (the gesture is entered only when
  `timer_swap_allowed()`, which needs `timer_extra_count() > 0`). The
  candidate cannot stick.
- The candidate passing the slot it started from is not special-cased in the
  loop: it is just another step, and counting continues past it. Only the
  final candidate matters. If it ends ON the start slot,
  `timer_select_slot` refuses (timer.c:224, "a lap that lands home is not a
  swap") and `wake_policy_swap_paint_owed` usually says no paint.
- The latch (main/button_latch.c `button_latch_record`) knows nothing of
  candidates; its three filters are release gate (needs a level sample, every
  20 ms poll is one), refractory (120 ms from the last accepted C press during
  the gesture; the seeded wake press starts at 300 ms) and settle (30 ms from
  the sample that saw the release). None depends on where the count is, so no
  press is dropped "at the wrap". Human taps at 150-1000 ms clear all three
  between counted taps, but NOT the tap right after the wake press as first
  written: the seed held 300 ms from arming while the guard changed only the
  next press's window, so a tap 120-300 ms after arming was refused. Fixed by
  Task 4 below (the guard restamps the seed); the claim now holds for that tap
  too, from 120 ms after arming.
- Budget cutoff commits whatever candidate it reached (test_swap7).

What the owner most likely sees as "stuck", all timing or design, none a
defect:

1. A tap that lands just after the quiet window has lapsed. The gesture has
   committed and the handler's stale-edge drain (wake_flow.c ~3962,
   `buttons_take_pressed`) has already run: it runs BEFORE the commit paint.
   A tap during the paint, the net_apply join, the join repaint and any
   chore-ack/event wait stays latched (none of those take C in Timers mode)
   and is discarded at deep sleep; the pad is up again by then, so it never
   wakes the device either. The count stops on an intermediate timer and the
   remaining taps are lost: "stuck" part way round. A wider quiet window
   (this change) shrinks the chance; the owner doc now says to wait for the
   LEDs to go out before pressing again.
2. Counting a whole lap: N taps with N enabled slots lands back on the start,
   which by design writes nothing and paints nothing. It looks like the
   button did nothing.
3. Taps made while the device is still waking (before the LEDs light) are not
   counted one by one (documented in docs/behavior/timers_and_schedule.md).

Existing coverage of the wrap: test_swap3 (last timer -> Screen -> next),
test_swap4 (lap home), test_swap7 (multi-lap under the budget, all slots).
None pins an ordinary (window-ended) count that passes the start slot and
keeps going, nor a press landing exactly as the window lapses. Added
test_swap3b for both.

## Task 4: review fixes (clicks-review.notes.md)

SHOULD-FIX 1, the tap after the wake press. The seed
(`button_latch_seed_press`, main/button_latch.c) stamps the 300 ms default at
arm time (`buttons_watch_begin`, main/buttons.c), and
`wake_flow_swap_press_guard(true)` changed only the configured window, so the
seed kept 300 ms. New `button_latch_restamp_running(btn)` re-stamps the
running window and settle from the configured figures, leaving anchors and
gate alone; `buttons_restamp_running` wraps it in the latch critical section,
and the guard calls it after installing 120/30 (counting only; putting the
default back does not restamp).

Bounce check, done before changing anything:
- Pad held at arming: `button_latch_begin` seeds (gate shut), the sample
  keeps it shut (`note_levels` held branch). No edge counts until a take's
  sample sees the pad up; the settle then runs from that sample, now 30 ms
  (restamped) instead of the seed's 10 ms. Same cover as any counted tap
  held past 120 ms (test_swap19). Pinned: test_swap17 (rewritten; the bounce
  20 ms after the reopening sample is refused, a tap at 400 counts) and
  test_button_latch `test_a_restamped_seed_judges_its_release_by_the_gesture_settle`.
- Pad up at arming: the release happened before arming; the arm sample
  anchors the settle at arming and the 120 ms window also runs from arming,
  so leftover ringing must outlast both. Pinned: test_swap17b.
- The old test_swap17 (edge 140 ms after an up-at-arming sample) pinned a
  stricter standard than any counted tap gets; at 140 ms past the release
  observation it is indistinguishable from a genuine tap. Rewritten as above.

Taps before buttons_init arms the ISR remain unobservable; the owner doc's
"start counting once the LEDs light" stays, with its figure now 120 ms.

New tests: test_swap16b (taps at 200/400/600 after arming count 4; failed
at 3 before the fix), test_swap17 (rewritten), test_swap17b; swap15 asserts
one restamp on C; test_button_latch: five `restamp` cases. NIT 5:
`_Static_assert(QUIET_MS * 1000 > BUTTON_LATCH_SWAP_REFRACTORY_US)` in
test_swap3b (the window that applies to the first tap after the fix).

SHOULD-FIX 2: owner-doc "Things to know" bullet added (taps after the count
ends are ignored until the device sleeps).
